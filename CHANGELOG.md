# Changelog

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-13

### Added

- Manual graphics and display controls in Graphics Profiles - Custom.
- Postprocessing, view distance, depth of field, lens flare, chromatic aberration, bloom, sharpening and fog controls.
- Saved custom effect overrides and exact restoration of the previous custom settings.
- Low labels for the additional quality groups in the original graphics menu.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-13

### Added

- Choose Balanced or Performance for lighting, shadows, reflections and effects.
- Restore previous returns these four groups to their original values.
